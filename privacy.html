Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026. In plain words: your research is yours.

What your account holds

Your name, your email address and your password (stored scrambled, so nobody can read it). We use your email to confirm your account, to send you a code if you forget your password, and nothing else. No newsletters unless you ask.

Your projects and files

Everything you make in SoCubes is saved on your Mac first. Your projects, PDFs, marks, notes, tasks and library files are also backed up to your SoCubes account so they appear on your other Macs. Only you can see them: the cloud checks every request against your account. We don’t read, sell or share them, and we don’t use them to train any AI.

What never leaves your Mac

  • Your Claude chats and your Anthropic key (the key is kept in your Mac’s Keychain).
  • Your browsing history in web panes.
  • Website logins you let SoCubes remember (also kept in the Keychain).
  • Your passcode, Touch ID and authenticator settings for locked projects.

Claude and other assistants

When you ask Claude something, your question and anything you chose to share (the page you’re reading, your notes, your library) go from your Mac straight to Anthropic, using your own key, under Anthropic’s own privacy terms. When you sign in to ChatGPT, Gemini or another assistant inside a pane, that’s their website and their privacy terms.

Who helps us run SoCubes

  • Supabase keeps accounts and the cloud backup.
  • Resend sends the confirmation and password emails.
  • Netlify hosts this website and delivers the messages you send us from the contact page (your name, email and message, only so we can reply).
  • GitHub stores the download files.
  • Google Fonts provides this website’s fonts.

No ads and no tracking: there are no analytics in the app or on this website.

Deleting everything

Delete your account in SoCubes under Settings › Delete account. Your account, your cloud backup and your files on that Mac are removed for good. Projects you delete stay in Recently deleted for 30 days, so you can change your mind, then they’re gone.

Questions
